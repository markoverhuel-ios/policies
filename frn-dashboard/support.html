Frequently asked questions

Where does the license data come from?

Directly from the FCC's public Universal Licensing System (ULS). The app doesn't maintain its own database. It fetches live data and caches it on your device for offline viewing.

Can I file a license or renewal through the app?

No. FRN Dashboard is read-only. It does not submit anything to the FCC. For filings and renewals, use the FCC's own ULS site.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

We don't back it up. All tracked FRNs, cached licenses, and settings are stored only on your device. There's no account and no server, so nothing syncs between devices. On Android, if device backup is on, Google may restore the app's data on a new phone. Otherwise nothing is recoverable if the app is deleted. Export your license list to CSV first if you need a copy.

Why is a license showing as “No Longer Active” but I still see it?

By default, expired, terminated, and cancelled licenses stay visible so you have a full record. Go to Settings to auto-hide them after 30, 90, 180, or 365 days.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same account you used to buy it: your Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or your Google account on Android (the one signed in to Google Play). Then use Restore Purchase in the app. Purchases don't transfer between iPhone and Android, because each store keeps its own.

The app isn't affiliated with the FCC. What does that mean?

FRN Dashboard is an independent tool built on top of publicly available FCC data. Not affiliated with the FCC or FAA.
