Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

HAMTrainer keeps your data on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and the app contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.

About the app

HAMTrainer is a study app for the FCC Amateur Radio Technician license exam.

Data stored on your device

  • Quiz attempts and session statistics. These are saved in the app's on-device storage (Apple's SwiftData on iPhone and iPad, a local database on Android), so the app can show your progress.
  • Trial start date. A single timestamp is saved locally so the app can run its free trial.
  • Android backup. On Android, if device backup is on, Google may back up this saved data to your Google Account so it can be restored on a new phone. That is controlled by your device settings and covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

The developer receives none of this data, and it is not uploaded to any server of ours. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK of any kind.

Purchases

Purchases and transaction records are handled entirely by Apple (App Store and StoreKit) on iPhone and iPad, and by Google (Google Play and Google Play Billing) on Android. HAMTrainer never sees or stores your payment information. Their handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing with third parties

No data is shared with or sold to third parties, because none is collected off your device in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
