HAMTrainer

Study app for the FCC Amateur Radio Technician license exam

OverviewPrivacySupport

HAMTrainer is a study app for the FCC Amateur Radio Technician exam. It carries the full 2026 to 2030 question pool and a written explanation for every question. It works without a connection.

Get support   Privacy policy

Features

  • The full NCVEC 2026 to 2030 Technician pool: 409 questions in 35 groups and 10 subelements
  • The pool's three reference diagrams, shown with the questions that use them
  • Quick Session, Full Pool, and One Per Topic practice
  • Timed exam simulation: 35 questions, one from each group, 26 correct to pass
  • Feedback right after each answer, or graded at the end
  • Practice everything, one subelement, or single groups
  • Stats tab with accuracy over time and your weakest topics
  • Browse tab to search the whole pool, with explanations

How it works

  1. Choose a practice mode.
  2. Choose a scope: all topics, a subelement, or single groups.
  3. Choose whether to see each answer right away or at the end.
  4. Answer the questions and read the explanation for each.
  5. Check the Stats tab to see which topics need more work.

Who it is for

Anyone studying for the entry-level amateur radio license.
