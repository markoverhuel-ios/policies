Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Fresh Coast Dashboard does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no account system and no backend server operated by the developer. This policy explains what the app stores and where it goes.

What the app stores

The FRNs you add, the license data fetched for them, your app settings, and cached map locations are stored locally on your device only, using the platform's standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device except as described below.

If device backup is on, Google may back up this saved data to your Google Account so it can be restored on a new phone. That is controlled by your device settings and covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

Data sent to the FCC and FAA

To retrieve license information, the app loads pages from the FCC's public Universal Licensing System (ULS) directly from your device, the same way a web browser would. The FRNs and call signs you search are sent to fcc.gov to retrieve the corresponding public license records. For antenna structures, the app also sends the structure's FAA study number to the FAA's public obstruction evaluation site (oeaaa.faa.gov) to retrieve the case status and any determination letters. These are the agencies' own public systems; the developer does not see or receive this traffic. See the FCC's privacy policy at fcc.gov for how they handle requests to their site. The FAA's privacy policy is available on faa.gov.

Data from our data server

The app downloads public FCC data from our data server (data.freshcoastrf.com, run on Cloudflare). There are two kinds of file:

  • Antenna structure data. To look up antenna structure registrations, the app downloads a public file of FCC antenna structure data. If our server is unavailable it falls back to the FCC's own server (data.fcc.gov).
  • License frequencies and locations. When you open a license, the app downloads the public frequency and site records for it from our server, instead of loading them from the FCC's site. The file requested is named by a group of about a thousand license numbers, so the request does not contain your FRN, your list of FRNs, or any call sign you searched. If a license is not in our files, the app loads that license's records from the FCC as described above.

These are ordinary web requests. Like any web server, Cloudflare receives your device's IP address with the request. We do not use it to identify you. No account or personal information is included in the request.

No third-party services

The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It uses Google's Android libraries, Google Play Billing for the purchase, and the open-source osmdroid map library. None of these collects data or sends it to us.

Notifications

If you enable license expiration reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device, through Android's notification system (Android 13 and later ask your permission). No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Location

The app displays license site locations from FCC public records on a map. It does not access or use your device's actual location. Map pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap's public tile servers, which receive your device's IP address and the map area you view, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.

Purchase

The app's purchase is handled by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

Data deletion

Removing an FRN from the app deletes its cached data from your device immediately. Deleting the app removes all stored data. Because there is no server or account, the developer has no copy of your data to delete on request.

Children's privacy

Fresh Coast Dashboard is a professional/technical tool not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
