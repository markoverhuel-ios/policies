Frequently asked questions

Where does the license data come from?

Directly from the FCC's public Universal Licensing System (ULS). The app doesn't maintain its own database. It fetches live data and caches it on your device for offline viewing.

Can I file a license or renewal through the app?

No. Fresh Coast Dashboard is read-only. It does not submit anything to the FCC. For filings and renewals, use the FCC's own ULS site.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

We don't back it up. All tracked FRNs, cached licenses, and settings are stored only on your device. There's no account and no server, so nothing syncs between devices. If device backup is on, Google may restore the app's data on a new phone. Otherwise nothing is recoverable if the app is deleted. Export your license list to CSV first if you need a copy.

Why is a license showing as “No Longer Active” but I still see it?

By default, expired, terminated, and cancelled licenses stay visible so you have a full record. Go to Settings to auto-hide them after 30, 90, 180, or 365 days.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the Google account you used to buy it (the one signed in to Google Play), then use Restore Purchase in the app.

The app isn't affiliated with the FCC. What does that mean?

Fresh Coast Dashboard is an independent app that displays public records published by the FCC and FAA. Some of that data is served from copies hosted by Fresh Coast RF, LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the FCC, the FAA or any government agency.
