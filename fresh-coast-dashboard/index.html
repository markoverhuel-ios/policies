Fresh Coast Dashboard

Independent tracker for your RF licenses, for Android

OverviewPrivacySupport

Fresh Coast Dashboard keeps track of your RF licenses in one place. Add your registration numbers (FRNs) and the app finds every license and registered antenna structure under them, then sorts them by status and expiration date. It reminds you before a license expires.

Fresh Coast Dashboard is an independent app by Fresh Coast RF, LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the FCC, the FAA or any government agency. It displays public records, and some of that data is served from copies hosted by Fresh Coast RF, LLC.

Features

  • Track one or more FRNs, each with a label such as a client name
  • Licenses sorted by status: active, renew soon, expiring soon, expired, and cancelled
  • Searchable license list with call sign, radio service, licensee, grant and expiration dates, and a link to the FCC page for each license
  • Call sign lookup for any call sign, including ones you do not track
  • Map of the licensed locations for your tracked licenses and your registered antenna structures
  • Structures list with height, type, address, FAA study number, and FAA determination
  • Reminders before each license expires, with a lead time you choose (30 days by default)
  • Home screen widgets, small and medium, showing how many licenses need attention
  • PDF license report and CSV export

How it works

  1. Add an FRN. It is saved on your phone only.
  2. The app looks up the licenses under that FRN and lists them by status.
  3. The app downloads the FCC's weekly public tower file and keeps the structures your FRNs own.
  4. Open a structure to see its FAA study. The app asks the FAA's public site for that study when you do.
  5. Results are saved on your phone, reminders are scheduled, and the widget updates.

Who it is for

Businesses, agencies, and radio shops that hold FCC licenses, and anyone who manages licenses for clients.
