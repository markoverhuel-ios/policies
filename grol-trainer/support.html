Frequently asked questions

Is this an official FCC app?

No. GROL Trainer is built independently and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the FCC. It uses the FCC's publicly available Element 1 and Element 3 question pools.

Is the unlock a subscription?

No. After the one-week free trial, the full version is a one-time purchase. There are no recurring charges.

How do I reset my trial?

You can't. The free trial is tied to your device.

What does the app cover?

All 743 questions from the Element 1 and Element 3 pools. There's a 20-question quick drill, a Simulated Exam that draws one random question per exam topic (the same way the real exam is assembled), and a full-pool mode. The app also shows statistics for each topic and has a searchable browser with a plain-language explanation for every question.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Your quiz history and statistics stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
