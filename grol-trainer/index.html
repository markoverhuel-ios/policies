GROL Trainer

Study app for the FCC General Radiotelephone Operator License exam

GROL Trainer is a practice app for the FCC General Radiotelephone Operator License exam. It carries all 743 questions from the official Element 1 and Element 3 pools, with a short explanation for each. It works without a connection.

Features

  • Element 1 (143 questions) and Element 3 (600 questions) from the official FCC pools
  • Quick 20, Full Pool, and Simulated Exam practice
  • Simulated exam draws one question per key topic: 24 for Element 1, 100 for Element 3
  • Practice a whole element or pick subelements and key topics, with a live question count
  • Feedback right after each answer, or graded at the end
  • Short explanation for every question, with abbreviations spelled out
  • The pool's reference diagrams shown with their questions
  • Stats with Today and Overall views, session history, and a suggested review list of your weakest topics
  • Browse every question by subsection, with tap-to-reveal answers

Explanations are study aids, not official answers.

How it works

  1. Choose an element and a practice mode.
  2. Choose the scope, or pick subelements and key topics.
  3. Choose whether to see each answer right away or at the end.
  4. Answer the questions and read the explanation for each.
  5. Check Stats for your suggested review list.

Who it is for

Technicians who need the FCC commercial operator license for aviation, marine, or two-way radio work.
