Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Spectrum Scout does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only on your device, to center the map. It is never sent anywhere. On Android you can say no and still use the app; the map and search work without it.

What the app connects to

  • Our data server (data.freshcoastrf.com, run on Cloudflare): to download public map data files (FCC license data). It receives a normal web request, like any website would. Older versions of the app download the same files from GitHub.
  • Apple Maps (iPhone and iPad): when you search for a place, your search text is sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap (Android): map pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap's public tile servers, and when you search for a place, your search text is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search service. They receive your device's IP address and the map area or text, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.
  • FAA (oeaaa.faa.gov): only when you open an FAA aeronautical study. Only the study number is sent.
  • Apple App Store or Google Play: to handle the in-app purchase. The developer never sees your payment details.

The "View location on CellMapper" button opens CellMapper's website in your browser. That site has its own privacy policy.

On your device

Downloaded map data, your filter choices and the free-trial start date are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them. On Android, if device backup is on, Google may back up your settings and trial date to your Google Account so they can be restored on a new phone. That is controlled by your device settings and covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
