Spectrum Scout

FCC towers and licensed sites on one map, for radio hobbyists

OverviewPrivacySupport

Spectrum Scout puts FCC antenna structures and licensed transmitter sites on one map. Tap a tower to see every license that names it, with frequencies, emission, and power. The data comes from public FCC and FAA records, rebuilt every week.

Features

  • Map of registered towers and licensed transmitter sites, grouped when zoomed out
  • Tower sheet listing every license on the tower, plus nearby sites that are not linked to it
  • Frequency, emission, output power, and ERP for each license, with a link to the FCC page
  • Microwave links drawn between sites
  • Filters by service, by band, or by a custom MHz range
  • Search by place, call sign, or ASR number, anywhere in the country
  • Long-press any spot to list the area licenses that cover it, and outline the market
  • Optional FAA layer for obstacles, with lighting and marking records and a live look at the FAA study
  • Export to CSV or a PDF tower report; areas you have browsed work offline

Data is as filed with the agencies and can be out of date. It does not include amateur or GMRS licenses.

How it works

  1. Pan and zoom the map, then tap "Search this area."
  2. We send your phone map data for that area. We rebuild the data every week.
  3. Tap a tower or site to see its licenses, frequencies, and owner.
  4. Long-press an empty spot to see area licenses, such as PCS, AWS, 700 MHz, and 3.5 GHz, that cover it.
  5. Areas you have browsed stay on your phone and keep working without a connection.

Who it is for

Radio hobbyists, tower spotters, scanner listeners, and RF technicians.
