Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Fresh Coast RF, LLC makes Tower Roster. It collects nothing about you.

  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • If you use "Find towers near me," your location is used on your device to list nearby towers. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your saved meter readings, recent towers and settings stay on your device.
  • The app downloads public FCC and FAA data from GitHub. That request does not include any personal information.
  • When you tap a link to an FCC record, it opens fcc.gov in Safari, which has its own policy.

Phone numbers shown in the app are public business and agency numbers from FCC records. Numbers for individuals are not included.
