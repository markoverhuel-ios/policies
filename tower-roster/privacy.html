Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Fresh Coast RF, LLC makes Tower Roster. It collects nothing about you.

  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • If you use "Find towers near me," your location is used on your device to list nearby towers. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your saved meter readings, recent towers and settings stay on your device. On Android, if device backup is on, Google may back up your settings to your Google Account so they can be restored on a new phone. That is controlled by your device settings and covered by Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.
  • The app downloads public FCC and FAA data from our data server (data.freshcoastrf.com, run on Cloudflare) or from GitHub. That request does not include any personal information.
  • When you tap a link to an FCC record, it opens fcc.gov in your browser (Safari on iPhone and iPad), which has its own policy. The "Search the Web" button opens a Google search for the licensee's phone number in your browser, and Google's policy applies there.
  • The one-time unlock purchase is handled by the App Store on iPhone and iPad and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details.
  • On Android, the app asks for location permission only for "Find towers near me." You can say no and still look up towers by number.

Phone numbers shown in the app are public business and agency numbers from FCC records. Numbers for individuals are not included.
