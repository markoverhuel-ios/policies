Support

Tower Roster is an aid, not a safety clearance. It uses public FCC and FAA databases that can be wrong, out of date or incomplete. Verify everything. You are responsible for your own RF safety.

Common questions

Where do I find the tower number?

It is the FCC registration (ASR) number, on the sign near the base of the tower or on your work order.

Why are cell carriers missing?

Most cell carrier equipment is not in the FCC records the app uses.

A phone number is wrong or missing.

Numbers come from the FCC's own filings. Correct them with the licensee or the FCC.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same account you used to buy the unlock: your Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or your Google account on Android (the one signed in to Google Play). Then tap Restore Purchase. Purchases don't transfer between iPhone and Android, because each store keeps its own.

How do I free up space?

Go to Settings and tap Delete Downloaded FCC Data.

The first lookup is slow.

It downloads about 27 MB of public data once. Use Wi-Fi.
